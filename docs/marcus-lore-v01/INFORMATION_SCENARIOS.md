# Reproducible information scenarios

Newly authored positive knowledge: today's signed counterfoil changes tomorrow's Contra collection to loading gate C, 07:00–07:30, docket R-17. Marcus has a collection to arrange and lacks that operative detail. New negative knowledge: counterfoil R-17 records six returned crates against ledger L-42's eight, a two-crate mismatch. It does not prove theft or who made an error. Marcus has relied on the signed summary as reconciled.

The opening reveals the player's exact information, not the hidden business-interest entries. Source verification shows a header/signature while covering the operative line; a relevance probe reveals only a possible use. Both scenarios allow immediate business, retention, early disclosure or withdrawal.

## Positive voluntary exchange

With `lore-3`, choose a non-antagonistic approach such as EA/BALANCED. VERIFY_SOURCE then PROBE_USEFULNESS establishes a checkable source and observed relevance without sharing the exact route. Propose a defensible two-unit credit arrangement with OFFER_INFORMATION. The resolver supplies +8 to the proposal score, subject to the engine's unchanged hard financial limits. Marcus may approve, counter or refuse; no stock, cash or exact information transfers during proposal.

If an offer results, CLARIFY_OFFER can preserve it under the engine's clarification rule. Confirm its exact identity to transfer the agreed cash/Contra and deliver the exact route. At rejection or withdrawal only the category has been exposed. Further proposals can still bundle the undelivered detail; its score contribution does not accumulate. Compare the same prepared state and same financial terms with NONE: there is no conditional +8. H4 independently verifies a matched change in integrated outcomes.

An optional longer route is SMALL_TALK, ACK_MISSED, VERIFY_SOURCE, PROBE_USEFULNESS, an initial DEAL with information, a genuinely improved DEAL with information, CLARIFY_OFFER, ACCEPT. For example, first propose two units with $30 upfront, $90 principal, no extra and ten days; then improve to $40 upfront, $80 principal, $4 extra and seven days. The first six ASK/DEAL exchanges have distinct purposes. A category hint after a usefulness probe is already-known content and supplies no fresh progress. The engine's finite patience allowance must accommodate the substantive route. Information mechanisms impose no universally required Vibe.

Early-disclosure comparison: DISCLOSE_FULL, VERIFY_SOURCE, PROBE_USEFULNESS, DEAL. Source and possible relevance can still be learned, but OFFER_INFORMATION is unavailable because Marcus already knows the route. The old debt and all economic limits remain unchanged. Full→verify→probe unit coverage checks that feedback acknowledges the already-disclosed detail and that its belief status becomes DOCUMENT_SUPPORTED without restoring private value.

## Negative bounded opening

With `lore-0`, prepare through VERIFY_SOURCE, PROBE_USEFULNESS and QUESTION_RECORD. These establish a genuine source, current ledger relevance and a specific question about the claimed reconciliation. DISCLOSE_FULL with a non-hostile approach at tension no higher than 45 gives Marcus the exact mismatch, revises reconciled certainty to a disputed record, and opens a temporary negotiation opportunity. It adds confidence 1 and tension 2: he is unsettled but has a concrete reason to consider finishing a deal while checking the record.

The first subsequent DEAL gets +6 only if it arrives within the next three turn positions, still has its authored grounds, uses a non-hostile approach, and tension remains at most 45. The first DEAL consumes the opening whether it succeeds, fails, or qualifies for no bonus. Waiting past expiry, or using a hostile first proposal, loses it. Repeated disclosure cannot reopen it. The player still needs real cash, valid stock quantity and defensible credit; embarrassment is not automatic consent.

Negative hostile classifier is an encounter-specific gate for this opportunity, separate from and not a replacement for the existing BASED table: BA, BS, BE, BD, AB, AD, DB, DA, DS, DE fail the prepared-use gate. The other ten Vibes remain possible, subject to actual social context. Intensity stays in the existing social interpretation; it cannot change the receipt or invent a threat.

Backlash comparison: disclose immediately without preparation, or prepare and use BE/OVERT for the disclosure. Marcus receives the exact fact but the resolver applies confidence -4 and tension +9, and no opening is created. Later source checks may support the document; they cannot replay a now-known revelation. This is ASK through a pointed question/disclosure, never “agree or I expose you.” The negative variant has no trade-for-silence option.

Unit coverage checks preparation, permanent knowledge, belief revision, expiry, hostile first-proposal consumption, repeat resistance and early/hostile backlash. Integrated policy/HTTP/browser result evidence is owned by H4 and H1; these described routes are not a substitute for their independent execution report.
