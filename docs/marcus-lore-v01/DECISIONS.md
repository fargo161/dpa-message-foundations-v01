# Integration decisions

- Build from audited 72451fa in an isolated worktree, locally on port 4175. The existing 4174 public prototype is separate. New cookie name marcus_lore_session avoids cross-port cookie collision. No tunnel or publication for this mission.
- Preserve exactly seven numeric gameplay metrics. Patience increases from 12 to 20 to fit evidence and six-plus substantive exchanges. Repeats still cost 1+min(repetitions,2), with no regeneration; clarification also consumes patience.
- Replace the blanket three-turn cutoff. First specific DEBT/RISK/FINAL_SAY acknowledgment, genuinely new information progress, first proposal, and a new record cash/unit/deadline concession can receive appropriate authored social interpretation. No positive reward for generic or exhausted questions, repeated facts, tone changes or oscillating terms. The canonical 20-Vibe table and SUBTLE/BALANCED/OVERT salience remain unchanged. Removed only the obsolete positiveTurnBudget configuration field.
- Information influences a derived proposal score; credit validity still separately enforces cash share, debt exposure, new principal and duration limits. Positive bundled counteroffers reduce additional repayment by at most 6 as explicit exchange terms. Price/principal equality never changes. Positive information is delivered only by the authoritative acceptance path. Negative information has no conditional-threat contract.
- CLARIFY_OFFER preserves exact offer identity/version and exchange, while request version advances. All other ASK/DEAL invalidate old offers. Exhausting patience during clarification ends negotiation and invalidates it. No arbitrary clarifying prose can change conditions.
- PRIORITIES supplies ambiguous shared concerns rather than an immediate quirk diagnostic. Specific reactions are defeasible clues. Known facts, observations and Marcus beliefs remain separate.
- Intentional baseline test revisions: expected patience after first turn is 19; a new specific acknowledgment can help after turn three; generic PRIORITIES is not the exact quirk clue; old generic/repetition anti-farming assertions remain. Economic, replay and semantic tests are retained.
- New provisional objective: at least two units with positive accepted principal. One-unit cash purchase remains valid and is reported as a limited cash outcome, not a forbidden action. Outcome quality is descriptive, not a hidden victory meter.
- Reconcile historical AGENTS status with reviewed authoring previews, retaining zero production-approved TPL protocols and all trusted adapter boundaries. New lore events are not counterfeit foundation resolutions.

- Review tightened concession eligibility: no-worse stock request, upfront cash, new principal and days relative to the immediately previous offer, plus a beneficial record versus all prior offers. A token cash increase cannot mask a much worse credit package. Category hints use one shared consumption key across probes, record questions and disclosure; late partial disclosure cannot create fresh progress.
- Source verification after full disclosure updates supported-content belief (and disputed-record belief where applicable), but cannot restore private value or open a spent negative opportunity. Play strips internal fact bindings from exchange objects; Debug retains them.
- The repository privacy gate disallows machine-specific user paths in candidate tracked files. Audit and tracked launch instructions are portable; exact local paths belong only in user-facing outputs. The historical baseline playtest file remains unchanged.
