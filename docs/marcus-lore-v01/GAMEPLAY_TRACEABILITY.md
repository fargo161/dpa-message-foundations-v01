# Gameplay traceability

The authoritative per-entry propositions, actor/target, context, validity, knowledge, provenance, interests, preconditions, repetition and continuity are in history-content.mjs. The table below maps each to actual executable consequences, not just dialogue. H4's `information: every meaningful lore fact has a present/absent gameplay pair` executes the paired comparisons; SCENARIO_RESULTS.json and VALIDATION_REPORT.md record independent outcomes.

| Entry | Actual consequence | Absence evidence / relevant acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OLD_ACCOUNT | Grounds DEBT/GUARANTEE and missed-check-in acknowledgment; the separate obligation remains 250 | DEBT/ACK_MISSED unavailable if missing, without deleting debt |
| STOCK_TITLE | Authorizes DEAL/ACCEPT and ownership/risk discussion | Missing title blocks valid purchase/acceptance without transfers |
| MISSED_CHECKIN | First specific ACK_MISSED creates progress and social response after turn three too | Absent history cannot authorize acknowledgment |
| SHARED_LOADING_SHIFT | Optional SMALL_TALK has a one-time cooperative basis | Absent shift blocks small talk; direct credit remains possible |
| DIRECT_RECEIPT | VERIFY_SOURCE creates checkable provenance evidence without operative detail | Missing receipt blocks verification and prepared benefits |
| LEDGER_CLOSING | Grounds current usefulness probe and both prepared information paths | Absent context removes the relevant option/bonus |
| POSITIVE_ROUTE | Player can disclose or offer private pickup instructions; accepted exchange delivers them | Absent detail blocks disclosure/exchange; early full revelation removes sale value |
| PICKUP_NEED | Makes checked collection information useful enough to bargain over | With no need, usefulness evidence is not granted and exchange stays unavailable |
| NEGATIVE_DISCREPANCY | Supports non-threatening contradiction and one bounded prepared next-DEAL opportunity | Without the detail there is no record-question/disclosure authorization |
| RECORD_ASSERTION | Establishes a stated reliance on the count, then a contestable belief | Missing assertion blocks pointed record question and prepared negative opening |

Positive matched example independently identified: seed `independent-lore-2`, VERIFY_SOURCE then PROBE_USEFULNESS with EA/BALANCED, proposal 2 units / 41 upfront / 79 principal / 0 extra / 7 days. Without an exchange it counters; with the private information it approves. Exact cash, principal and old debt remain unchanged until explicit acceptance.

Negative prepared sequence: VERIFY_SOURCE, PROBE_USEFULNESS, QUESTION_RECORD, DISCLOSE_FULL, then a defensible DEAL within three subsequent turn positions. The opening contributes at most +6 to one proposal score; it cannot bypass hard limits. Unprepared or hostile disclosure gives backlash and cannot be replayed into a new opening. Exact matched numbers/seeds are recorded by H4.

Cross-cutting evidence: six-plus meaningful exchanges, a real late concession, optional direct business, limited cash purchase versus intended credit, disclosure continuity, absent-fact gates, rejected malformed input, replay/foreign-session isolation, current-offer clarification, and terminal reaction continuity are independently tested. Generic clues remain imperfect. None of these mechanics implement PRESSURE, full TPL or facial slots.
