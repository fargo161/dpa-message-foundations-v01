## 1. Mission

Create the first clean, expandable foundation for DPA communication authoring.

The milestone is to research, select, define, implement, and demonstrate **10–20 interconnected, highly combinatorial semantic keywords** grounded in:

- everyday human event logic;
- material relationships and resource dependencies;
- obligations, promises, permissions, ownership, access, and control;
- directed relationships and social roles;
- knowledge, belief, secrets, exposure, and information asymmetry;
- trust, fear, respect, resentment, familiarity, dependence, and emotional leverage;
- active context, history, blockers, and defeaters.

The keywords must produce mechanically meaningful combinations that expose concrete semantic actions under:

```text
DEAL
PRESSURE
ASK
```

Those actions must connect cleanly to:

```text
20 ordered BASED Vibes
x 3 speech acts
x 3 Delivery Intensities
= exactly 180 canonical performance coordinates
```

This is the semantic and structural foundation for the DPA Message Builder and TPL Intake Library. It is not the large dialogue-population pass.

## 2. Canonical synthesis law

> Lore establishes the situation. Mechanics resolve the move. BASED establishes the performance. TPL writes the performance. The face supplies separate supporting evidence.

Runtime order:

```text
authored lore + live state
    -> relevant relationship/context/material facts
    -> semantic keyword graph
    -> action availability
    -> immutable state-transition contract
    -> semantic outcome resolution
    -> ordered BASED Vibe + Delivery Intensity
    -> 180-cell compatibility coordinate
    -> deterministic TPL realization
    -> structured semantic-invariance validation
    -> written message and separate expression request
```

External human-logic data may suggest candidates and explain authoring decisions. It may not bypass authored mechanics or mutate live state.

## 4. Player and semantic boundaries

The player selects only:

1. one hard-authored semantic request/action;
2. one ordered BASED Vibe; and
3. one Delivery Intensity: `SUBTLE`, `BALANCED`, or `OVERT`.

The player never selects raw TPL evidence, families, atoms, protocols, punctuation rules, face slots, hidden states, numeric Cue mixtures, success probability, or the legacy 70/30 convention.

The dialogue string is never mechanical truth. The semantic system owns:

- actor and target;
- operator and concrete action frame;
- resources, quantities, values, deadlines, conditions, permissions, prohibitions, and consequences;
- relationship, context, knowledge, belief, and stat facts;
- preconditions, outcome resolution, effects, and history emissions;
- mandatory facts and forbidden semantic additions.

BASED and TPL may change performance only. They cannot change the proposition or outcome.

## 8. Keyword ontology requirements

A keyword is a stable, compact semantic predicate or event/state operator. It is not a surface word, emotion, Vibe, personality label, prompt fragment, or finished sentence.

Select 10–20 final keywords only after research and a documented selection rubric. Illustrative candidates such as `OWES`, `NEEDS`, `CONTROLS`, `PROMISED_TO`, `TRUSTS`, `FEARS`, `RESENTS`, `DEPENDS_ON`, `KNOWS_SECRET_ABOUT`, and `HAS_LEVERAGE_OVER` are hypotheses, not mandatory names.

The final set must jointly cover:

1. material possession, ownership, shortage, need, access, or control;
2. obligation, debt, promise, permission, prohibition, or entitlement;
3. relationship role or dependence;
4. knowledge, belief, secrecy, evidence, or exposure;
5. emotional/social leverage such as trust, fear, respect, resentment, familiarity, protection, or vulnerability;
6. context, history, conditions, blockers, or defeaters.

Each keyword definition must include at least:

```text
keyword_id
display_name
definition
explicit_non_meanings
category
arity
typed_argument_roles
directionality
inverse_or_reciprocal_rule
allowed_entity_and_resource_types
temporality_and_history_behavior
negation_dispute_and_belief_handling
required_and_forbidden_contexts
related_stats
compatible_keywords
conflicting_keywords
possible_DEAL_PRESSURE_ASK_affordances
blockers_and_defeaters
possible_history_emissions
source_and_project_provenance
positive_examples
boundary_examples
counterexamples
schema_version
review_status
```

Do not encode whole sentences as keywords. Do not create synonyms that add no mechanic. Do not collapse relationship facts, scalar stats, contexts, and semantic actions into one field.

### Combinatorial quality gates

- Every keyword must participate in at least two meaningful cross-keyword rules or chains unless a documented semantic reason prevents it.
- Demonstrate at least three chains containing three or more keywords.
- Include directional and asymmetric cases.
- Include contradiction, blocker, expired/history, false-belief, and insufficient-knowledge cases.
- Enumerate valid configurations deterministically and remove duplicates.
- Report theoretical combinations separately from valid, reachable, unique configurations.
- Demonstrate at least 100 valid unique semantic configurations across the supplied fixtures if the reviewed ontology supports them. If it does not, report the verified count and exact missing dimensions; never pad the total.

## 9. Semantic actions and mechanics

Implement a distinction between:

- **macro operator:** `DEAL`, `PRESSURE`, or `ASK`;
- **concrete semantic action:** for example `REQUEST_EXTENSION`, `OFFER_CASH_FOR_EXTENSION`, `TRADE_INFORMATION`, or `INVOKE_AUTHORED_CONSEQUENCE`;
- **bound payload:** actor, target, resources, quantity, value, deadline, condition, leverage, permission, consequence, recipient, location, and applicable facts;
- **resolved state-transition contract:** precondition evaluation, outcome, deterministic effects, history, mandatory semantic facts, and forbidden additions.

Action availability must arise only from authored rules over keyword assertions, context, knowledge, relationship facts, and state. Imported priors may be displayed as suggestions but cannot expose an action by themselves.

Initial fixtures must include:

- a material/debt scenario based on Marcus “Broker” Hill and Apartment 305;
- an access/permission scenario;
- an information/secret/leverage scenario.

Each scenario must prove multiple legal actions, at least one blocked action, and an explainable trace.

## 10. BASED matrix integration

Load the canonical project definitions for exactly five BASED Cues and 20 ordered two-Cue Vibes. The first Cue governs and the second modifies. `BA` and `AB` are different. Never infer definitions merely from the letters.

Canonical public intensities are exactly:

```text
SUBTLE
BALANCED
OVERT
```

Intensity controls written-signal salience. It does not determine urgency, volume, success, truth, coercion, outcome, personality, stat changes, or numeric Cue weights. The legacy 70/30 convention has no semantic authority.

Generate exactly one stable matrix record for every:

```text
20 ordered Vibes x 3 acts x 3 intensities = 180
```

Recommended key:

```text
{ACT}_{VIBE}_{INTENSITY}
```

Each cell must support:

```text
vibe_id
speech_act
delivery_intensity
action_invariant
candidate_anchor_ids
universal_fallback_id when approved
allowed_protocol_ids
preferred_protocol_ids
excluded_protocol_ids
required_context_or_lore_facts
forbidden_semantic_additions
review_status
provenance
```

Import all 180 supplied authored lines as project-authored candidate anchors. Do not automatically approve them as universal fallbacks. Audit them for unauthored claims involving audiences, friendship, deadlines, alternative offers, vulnerability, norms, knowledge, protection, threats, consequences, or leverage. Contextual lines must declare exact fact gates. Lines that cross from ASK into PRESSURE or otherwise drift acts must remain blocked or be revised in a later authorized pass.

The matrix structure and coverage view are in scope. Large-scale dynamic dialogue variation is deferred.

## 12. Semantic invariance

Use structured slot checks, not vague text similarity.

Reject any candidate realization that:

- removes or changes a required slot;
- changes actor, target, recipient, object, quantity, price, deadline, ownership, location, permission, or condition;
- changes the macro speech act;
- invents or removes a threat, promise, accusation, concession, norm, audience, friendship, knowledge claim, leverage source, or consequence;
- asserts facts unavailable to the speaker;
- reveals author-only information;
- contradicts the resolved outcome.

Provide machine-readable rejection reasons and deterministic safe fallback behavior.
